ENGINEERED FOR ROAD TRIPS

Built on a "Journey, Not Permanent Tracking" Model

No family networks, no permanent surveillance feeds, and no social clutter. Location sharing starts when you press Start Journey, and turns off automatically when you arrive.

One Destination, Many Origins

The journey creator picks one common destination. Every friend chooses their own independent starting location and mode of travel (Car, Motorbike, or Walking).

Native Kotlin Foreground Service

Runs reliably under Android 14+ with an Adaptive Sampling Policy: 2.5s for fast highway movement, 5s for city driving, and 25s stationary heartbeats to preserve your phone battery.

Offline Maps & SQLite Resilience

Download vector map packs before remote areas. Continue on cached OSRM turn navigation, and queue offline GPS fixes in SQLite until cellular signal recovers.

Convoy Coordination & Chat

Built-in encrypted group chat, quick status chips (Fuel Stop, Food Stop, Heavy Traffic, Break), and 1-tap direct phone dialer access to any convoy participant.

Emergency Assistance (112 / SOS)

One-touch emergency action sheet. Broadcasts your live GPS coordinates immediately to your convoy and triggers direct emergency dialing to 112 / 108 medical response.

Indefinite History & RDP Compression

While live location sharing is temporary, completed road trips are retained indefinitely with 87%+ Ramer–Douglas–Peucker route polyline compression for route replays.

FRICTIONLESS WORKFLOW

Get on the Road in 3 Simple Steps

01

Create a Journey

Pick a destination (e.g. Goa Airport) and set the journey expiry (6h, 12h, 24h). SyncPoint generates a unique 6-character code (like #GOA42K) and invite link.

02

Invite Your Friends

Share the invite code or QR code with up to 9 friends. Each traveler opens the app, picks their starting point and travel mode, and gathers in the pre-trip lobby.

03

Hit Start Journey

Start tracking whenever you are ready. Every participant sees the live convoy map with real-time speeds, turn guidance, and arrival notifications at the 120m threshold.
